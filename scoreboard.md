# scoreboard.md (Valor Church)

The shared staff-meeting scoreboard and metric language. Use these section names, metric names, and phrases in any staff-meeting prep, agenda draft, recap, or scoreboard pull. Do not invent parallel terminology.

> **Purpose:** Protect the load-bearing systems of Sunday. Eliminate silos. Measure what matters. Drive momentum.
> **Cadence:** 90 minutes. Weekly. Non-negotiable.
> **Closing test:** *If 100 new people walked in this Sunday, would our SundayOS hold?* If no, that becomes next week's work.

*Note on numbers: the metric names below are shared. The actual giving and budget figures are held by Justin and Glenn and are not in the shared canon. Reference the metric; get the number from them.*

---

## The Eight Checkpoints (90 min total)

### 1. Opening Frame / Vision Alignment (5 min)
Justin only. Directional clarity, not a sermon. What is the spiritual emphasis of this season? What must not drift? What outcome are we reinforcing this month?

### 2. Sunday By the Numbers (15 min)
Data first. No opinions first.

**Attendance and engagement:** Total Attendance, First-Time Guests, Salvations / Baptisms, Kids Attendance, Online Views.

**Systems metrics:** % Volunteer Coverage, First Step Attendance *(Valor = Meet the Pastor)*, % First Step to Team, Guest Capture Rate, Follow-Up Completion Rate.

**Financial snapshot:** Weekly Giving and YTD Trend vs. Budget are reviewed here by Justin and Glenn. The figures are not in this file.

*Discussion prompts: Where did we outperform? Where did we leak? What system is under stress?*

### 3. Campus / Service Sunday Review (15 min)
Structural review, not storytelling. For each service: Did we hit the defined Sunday outcome? Where did friction show up? What broke under pressure? Were volunteers fully covered? Was the response moment clear? Was First Step visible and compelling? **Output: 1 win + 1 structural improvement. Max 2 action items per service.**

### 4. Load-Bearing Systems Audit (15 min)
Which of these showed stress this week? Run-of-show execution, volunteer scheduling, First Step clarity, guest capture, follow-up rhythm, sermon planning pipeline, production transitions. **Pressure test:** *If attendance increased 20% next Sunday, what would fail first?* Assign one owner.

### 5. 30-60-90 Implementation Tracker (15 min)
Walk the roadmap: What is complete? What is delayed? What needs support? What must be prepared now for the 60-day build? Are the KPIs trending up, flat, or slipping? **No new initiatives until load-bearing systems are stable.**

### 6. Upcoming Sundays and Calendar Alignment (15 min)
Upcoming series and key dates (theme alignment, creative needs, production deadlines, volunteer load). Special events impacting Sunday. **Filter question:** *Does this support Sunday, or distract from it?*

### 7. Staffing and Leadership Development (10 min)
Open roles, high-potential leaders, volunteer lead gaps, burnout risk, succession pipeline. **Key question:** *Where are we leader-thin?* If you feel pressure, it is usually a leadership-density problem.

### 8. Decisions and Clear Next Steps (10 min)
Before leaving the room, every decision gets four answers: **What** is the decision (top 3 only)? **Who** owns it? **What** is the deadline? **What metric** proves it worked? No vague commitments.

### Closing Question (5 min)
*If 100 new people walked in this Sunday, would our SundayOS hold?* If no, that is next week's work.

## Cadence

- **Sunday 5 PM.** Slack ping with reflection prompts.
- **Monday.** 90-minute Executive Team meeting walks the eight checkpoints. (See the weekly rhythm in STAFF-ONBOARDING.md: the meeting runs 8:30-10:00, some in person and some on Zoom.)
- **First Monday of the month.** Add a +30 min deep dive on one system (volunteer pipeline, guest-to-First-Step conversion, production, campus health, or financial sustainability).

Use this file whenever you prep the Monday agenda: walk the eight checkpoints in order, surface the Load-Bearing Systems Audit and the 30-60-90 Tracker even when quiet, and log every §8 decision with an owner, a deadline, and a success metric.
