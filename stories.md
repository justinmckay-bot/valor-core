# stories.md (Valor Church shared canon)

Curated, share-safe stories from Valor's history and present. Source for marketing, social, sermon prep, communications, and any moment a teammate needs a true story to anchor copy.

This is the share-safe subset. Justin maintains a wider private STORIES.md inside his own workspace. When a moment requires a story not found here, ask Justin directly. Do not extrapolate or reconstruct from memory.

---

## How to use

Pull a story when copy needs one. Match by tag.

**Always check the consent line before public use.** Some stories are public-with-care, meaning the story is shareable but a specific name, organization, or detail must not be named. Read the consent block carefully.

If a story is being used publicly, log the use back to Justin so he can keep the master tracker honest about repeat fatigue.

---

## Consent levels in this file

- **Public.** Story can be told publicly with full names. No further check needed.
- **Public-with-care.** Story is shareable. Specific guardrails are spelled out in the consent line. Honor them strictly.
- **Public with edits applied.** The version below is already redacted. Sensitive details (specific names, financial terms) have been removed for share-safe use. Use as written. Do not add back the redacted detail.

### Story 01. "Where are the churches?"

- **When:** 2015 (Justin and Lacy's honeymoon in Candelas)
- **Tags:** origin, calling, Candelas, vision, capital campaign
- **Consent:** Public

In 2015, Justin and Lacy honeymooned in the area now known as Candelas in NW Arvada. Standing in a place full of new neighborhoods and growing families, Justin asked one question.

### Story 06. Kent and the Naming

- **When:** Pre-launch, before the February 2026 rebrand

Kent Shaw has planted more than 200 churches. He is Justin's coach and mentor. In conversation with Justin, Kent named two things that would shape everything. ### Story 07. Russ Sends Out

- **When:** 2026 (mid-July last Sunday leading worship; late July official send-out)
- **Tags:** sending, multiplication, worship, planting, Acts 13
- **Consent:** Public-with-care. Russ's send-out is public. Treat his family transition with pastoral respect.

Russ Daly came to Valor as worship leader and church planting resident. He stayed long enough to lead worship through the rebrand from The Local Church to Valor. He left to plant Golden City Church in Broomfield. His final Sunday leading worship was mid-July 2026; the official send-out came in late July.

Sending hurts. Sending is the point.

A church that does not send is not following the pattern Acts 13 names. Russ leaves not because Valor failed him but because Valor finished its work in him. He goes with the blessing of the elders and the prayers of the congregation. Golden City is the next step in a thread Valor has been pulling on since the beginning.

### Story 08. How Russ Came to Valor

- **When:** TLC era, when Russ was brought on as interim worship leader and the first church planting resident
- **Tags:** Russ, calling, providence, family, worship, planting, multiplication
- **Consent:** Public-with-care. The story is shareable. **Do not name the local megachurch Russ left.** **Do not name Justin's mother-in-law by name.** Treat his family transition with pastoral respect.

Russ Daly was on staff at a local megachurch on the Front Range. He walked away. Not because anything blew up. Because he was longing for more.

Justin's mother-in-law met Russ at a park one day. She heard a little of his story and said, *You should meet my son-in-law.* Russ already knew Justin. They had met before. They reconnected.

Valor brought Russ on as interim worship leader and the first church planting resident. He is part of a different network than Valor, but he shares the same DNA. His heart for the lost is plain. He is planting for the right reasons.

This is how Valor was already preparing to multiply, and how Russ was already being prepared for the plant. Two threads pulling toward the same room. The room they met in was a park. The matchmaker was a mother-in-law.
