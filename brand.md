# brand.md (Valor Church)

The non-negotiables. Apply these to every visual, printed, social, and digital asset.

## Naming

- Full name: **Valor Church**
- Short form: **Valor** (acceptable in casual or repeated reference)
- Former name: **The Local Church (TLC)**, used through February 2026
- Never write: Ballard, Ballard Church, or any voice-to-text corruption

## Venue

- Always **Excel Academy** or **Excel Academy Charter School**
- Never "Excel Charter Academy"
- Address: **11500 W 84th Ave, Arvada, CO 80005**
- Service time: **Sundays at 10a**

## Digital

- URL: **valor.church** (always lowercase)
- Social handle: **@myvalorchurchco**
- Partner site: **partnerwithvalor.church**

## Fonts

- **Archivo Black** for display and headlines
- **PT Serif** for body and supporting copy
- No other fonts. Ever.

## Color palette

| Color | Hex |
|---|---|
| Black | #000000 |
| Burgundy | #4D3033 |
| Khaki | #9F936B |
| Slate | #3B4A50 |

No other colors in primary brand assets. Photography and imagery do not need to match these, but typographic and graphic elements must.

## The four pillars in design

When listed visually, always in this exact order:

1. Unapologetic Preaching
2. Unashamed Worship
3. Unafraid Witness
4. Unceasing Prayer

## The motto

**All for Him. All Excellent. All In.**

Render as three lines stacked, or as one line with periods between each phrase. Never with em dashes between the phrases.

## What we do not do in design

- No generic AI aesthetic. No stock-feeling gradient swirls. No over-rounded sans-serif "modern church" templates.
- No em dashes in any copy on any visual asset. Use a period, a comma, or two short sentences.
- No mixing fonts outside Archivo Black and PT Serif.
- No watercolor, no pastel washes, no gradient millennial pinks.
- No softening the four pillars into something less direct.

## When in doubt

Black, burgundy, khaki, slate. Archivo Black, PT Serif. Excel Academy, Sunday at 10a, valor.church. The four pillars in order. That is the brand.
