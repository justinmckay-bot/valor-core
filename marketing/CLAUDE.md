# Marketing Workspace

This workspace handles external digital reach to non-attenders. Internal congregation-facing comms live in [communications/](../communications/). The root CLAUDE.md applies in full. Rules below layer on top.

## Scope

- Instagram, YouTube, Facebook
- WordPress (valor.church)
- Sunday morning photography
- Meta Ads and Google Ads
- SEO and GEO (local search optimization)
- Mailers (physical, when run)

## Primary collaborators

- Chris Jones (Marketing Associate) runs point on social and ads
- Kate Wojick handles occasional blog writing and Google Business Profile work

## Cadence

- Social on a weekly schedule that survives Justin's heavy sermon weeks
- YouTube post-Sunday (sermon upload with description)
- Ads ongoing, with monthly performance review
- Photography reviewed and tagged the week after each Sunday

## Brand rules (non-negotiable)

- Fonts: Archivo Black and PT Serif only
- Palette: #000000, #4D3033 (burgundy), #9F936B (khaki), #3B4A50 (slate)
- Venue: always "Excel Academy" or "Excel Academy Charter School"
- Address: 11500 W 84th Ave, Arvada, CO 80005
- Service time: Sundays at 10a
- Social handle: @myvalorchurchco
- URL: valor.church (lowercase, always)
- Four pillars in this exact order: Unapologetic Preaching, Unashamed Worship, Unafraid Witness, Unceasing Prayer
- Motto: All for Him. All Excellent. All In.
- Tagline: Stop Drifting. Build Resilient Faith.

## Voice rules (hard)

- No em dashes, ever
- No generic AI aesthetic in any visual output
- No jargon: no "faith journey," "gospel-centered," "intentional community," "curated"
- No corporate language: not "we are excited to announce," not "we invite you to join us"
- Story first, thesis second
- Peer-to-peer pastoral tone, never preachy at strangers
- Captions earn the click, then the click earns the trust
