## Sermon → Social Pipeline

**Owner:** Justin (directs all marketing). Chris Jones executes the weekly sermon upload only.
**Cadence:** Weekly, kicks off Sunday after service
**Source feed:** Resi (Sunday service capture)
**Tooling:** Resi, YouTube, podcast hosts, Sermon Clips (AI Reels generator, similar to Opus), Instagram, TikTok

### Step 1 — Sermon upload (Sunday/Monday)

- Pull the finished sermon file from Resi.
- Upload to YouTube as the canonical version, with description generated via the YouTube description skill.
- Push to Valor's other podcast accounts in the same pass so audio and video go live together.

### Step 2 — Reel extraction (Monday/Tuesday)

- Run the new sermon through Sermon Clips.
- Curate at least three strong Reels from the AI output. "Strong" means a clear hook, a complete thought, and a payoff inside the clip — not a teaser that requires the full sermon to make sense.
- Tag each Reel with sermon title, date, and a one-line hook so the bank stays searchable.

### Step 3 — This week's post (mid-week)

- Pick the single strongest Reel from the new sermon.
- Post to Instagram that week as the primary "fresh sermon" Reel.
- Hold the remaining two-plus Reels in the bank for future use.

### Step 4 — Filler cadence (rolling, all week)

- Alongside the new IG Reel, post Reels from previous sermons as filler content on YouTube (Shorts) and TikTok.
- Goal: the new-sermon Reel anchors IG that week, while older-sermon Reels keep YouTube and TikTok feeds active so the channels do not go quiet during heavy sermon-prep weeks.
- Pull filler from the bank built up in Step 2 across prior weeks.

### Standing rules

- Brand and voice rules in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) apply to every caption, title, and description.
- Pull stories for captions from [../STORIES.md](../STORIES.md). Check the consent line before public use.
- No em dashes in any caption, title, or description that goes out publicly.
- Captions earn the click, then the click earns the trust.

### Success looks like

- One fresh IG Reel from the most recent sermon every week.
- A growing bank of at least 2 unused Reels per sermon for future filler.
- YouTube and TikTok never go more than a few days without a new Reel, even during Justin's heavy sermon weeks.
