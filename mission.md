# mission.md (Valor Church)

## One sentence purpose

Valor exists to make resilient disciples who follow Jesus, build His Church, and refuse to drift.

## Tagline

**Stop Drifting. Build Resilient Faith.**

## Motto

**All for Him. All Excellent. All In.**

## The four pillars

In this exact order. Always.

1. **Unapologetic Preaching**
2. **Unashamed Worship**
3. **Unafraid Witness**
4. **Unceasing Prayer**

The pillars came out of conversation between Justin and Kent Shaw, Justin's coach and mentor who has planted more than 200 churches. They are non-negotiable. They shape every series, every gathering, every staff hire.

For the scriptural anchors of each pillar, see `convictions.md` Section 2.

## Founding logic

Valor began as **The Local Church (TLC)** in 2022. In February 2026, the church relaunched as **Valor Church** at Excel Academy in Arvada, Colorado. The name comes from the Hebrew *Chayil*: courage fused with virtue, rising generation to generation.

Valor's permanent home will sit at **15350 SH-72 in Candelas, Northwest Arvada**. The capital campaign funding that build-out is **The Unstoppable Initiative**.

## Who Valor serves

The **Five Parks neighborhood** and the surrounding NW Arvada community. Drifting families. People who grew up in church and walked away, or never were in one to begin with, and now feel the thinness of life without God.

## What Valor refuses to be

- Soft on hard truths to keep the room comfortable.
- Apologetic about the Bible, doctrine, or the gospel.
- A church built around a personality instead of around Christ and His mission.
- A church that does not send. Sending is the point.
